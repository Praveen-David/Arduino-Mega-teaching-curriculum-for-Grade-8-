# Capstone-D: Build Log Template

## Arduino Mega Capstone — Daily Build Log

> Your Build Log is a **scientific journal**. Real engineers and scientists keep detailed records of every step — what they tried, what worked, what broke, and how they fixed it. This log is a graded deliverable. Short vague entries lose marks. Specific detailed entries earn them.

---

**Project Name:** _______________________________________________
**Team Name:** _________________________________________________
**Team Members:** _________________________________ / _________________________________

---

## How to Fill In Each Entry

- Write entries **during or immediately after** each build session (not the day before the presentation!)
- Be **specific** — "wired the DHT11 to pin 4" is better than "did some wiring"
- **Problems are expected** — document them honestly; debugging is good science
- If you took a photo or sketch, note it in the "Photo / Sketch" space
- Every team member fills in the **Time & Contribution Log** at the bottom

*Teacher note: Build Log entries are worth 30% of the capstone grade. Look for specificity (names components, states exact pin numbers, quotes exact error messages), honesty (problems are described, not glossed over), and scientific thinking (the entry reads like a lab notebook, not a summary). Blank or vague entries at any level should be addressed early — prompt teams with "describe exactly what you did, not just that you did something."*
