# Capstone — Final Presentation Rubric 🏆

**Team / Project:** ____________________  **Members:** ____________________  **Date:** ____________

This rubric grades your **final capstone presentation and project**. It covers five areas. Each is scored 1–4. There is a **self-assessment** column so you can grade yourself first — be honest and reflective!

**Levels:** 1 = Beginning · 2 = Developing · 3 = Proficient · 4 = Exemplary

---

## The Rubric

### 1. Science Concept (×2 weight)
| 1 — Beginning | 2 — Developing | 3 — Proficient | 4 — Exemplary |
|---------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| Science idea unclear or missing | Names a science concept but explains it loosely | Clearly explains the science concept and how the project demonstrates it | Explains the science deeply and accurately, with correct vocabulary, and connects it to the wider world |

*Self: ___ / Teacher: ___*

### 2. Engineering Design
| 1 — Beginning | 2 — Developing | 3 — Proficient | 4 — Exemplary |
|---------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| Device doesn't work or is incomplete | Works partly; build is fragile or messy | Device works reliably; circuit is neat and well-planned | Robust, creative design; solves a real problem elegantly; shows iteration/improvement |

*Self: ___ / Teacher: ___*

### 3. Code Quality
| 1 — Beginning | 2 — Developing | 3 — Proficient | 4 — Exemplary |
|---------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| Code missing or doesn't compile | Code runs but is messy / barely commented | Clean, working code with helpful comments; logic is clear | Efficient, well-organized, fully commented; uses functions/libraries well; handles edge cases |

*Self: ___ / Teacher: ___*

### 4. Presentation Skills
| 1 — Beginning | 2 — Developing | 3 — Proficient | 4 — Exemplary |
|---------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| Hard to follow; reads from notes; very quiet | Some structure; uneven speaking; audience loses thread | Clear, organized, audible; both partners contribute; good visuals/demo | Engaging and confident; excellent demo; handles Q&A well; everyone speaks meaningfully |

*Self: ___ / Teacher: ___*

### 5. Reflection
| 1 — Beginning | 2 — Developing | 3 — Proficient | 4 — Exemplary |
|---------------|----------------|----------------|----------------|
| No reflection on process or results | Mentions one thing that went well/wrong | Thoughtfully reflects on what worked, what didn't, and the data/results | Deep reflection: analyzes results vs hypothesis, evaluates design honestly, proposes specific next steps |

*Self: ___ / Teacher: ___*

## Student Self-Reflection (complete before presenting)
1. What part of our project are we **proudest** of, and why?
2. What was our biggest **challenge**, and how did we solve it?
3. If we had one more week, what would we **improve**?
4. What did each team member **learn** that they didn't know before?
