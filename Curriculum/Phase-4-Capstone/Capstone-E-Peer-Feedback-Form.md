---

## How to Give Great Feedback
- Be **specific** — say *what* and *where*, not just "it's good."
- Be **kind** — comment on the work, never the person.
- Be **helpful** — give an idea they could actually use.
- Start with strengths (the "stars"), then offer one improvement (the "wish").

## ✂️ Quick Version — Gallery Walk Sticky Notes
*(For a fast "gallery walk," each reviewer leaves TWO sticky notes at each project station.)*

- 🟢 **Green note = a STAR:** "I like how you ______."
- 🟡 **Yellow note = a WISH:** "Have you thought about ______?"

**Rules for the gallery walk:**
1. Walk quietly; look and read before writing.
2. Every project gets at least one green and one yellow note.
3. No "bad," "boring," or unkind words — be a coach, not a critic.
