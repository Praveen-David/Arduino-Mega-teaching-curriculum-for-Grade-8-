# Arduino Mega for Young Engineers — Grade 8 STEM Curriculum

A complete, **18-week, semester-long Arduino Mega 2560 curriculum** for Grade 8 students (ages 13–14) with **no prior electronics or coding experience**. Students learn electronics fundamentals and coding while using engineering to demonstrate core Grade 8 science concepts.

- **36 sessions** (2 × 45 min per week × 18 weeks)
- **4 phases:** Foundations → Sensors & Analog Signals → Systems & Control → Capstone Science Project
- Every session includes: teacher guide, circuit diagram, fully-commented Arduino code, student worksheet, safety notes, assessment rubric, and differentiation.

## 🔧 Hardware

Arduino Mega 2560, breadboards, LEDs, resistors, capacitors, pushbuttons, potentiometers, LDRs, temperature sensors (DHT11/LM35), ultrasonic sensors (HC-SR04), servo motors, 16×2 I2C LCD, buzzers, jumper wires. Full inventory in the Course Overview.

## 🧪 Science Integrated Across the Semester

Ohm's Law & circuit theory · analog vs digital signals · electromagnetic spectrum/light · heat & thermodynamics · sound waves · mechanical motion & energy · data collection & graphing · feedback loops & systems thinking · the scientific method throughout.

## 🦺 Safety First

All projects run at **5V (no shock hazard)**. The class mantra: **Build → Check → Power on** — wire only when unpowered. See the Safety Policy in the Course Overview.
